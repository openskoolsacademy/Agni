# Apex Cloud Services - Knowledge Base & Policies

## 1. Company Overview
Apex Cloud Services provides high-performance cloud hosting, managed Kubernetes, and AI-powered data analytics for enterprise customers. Founded in 2021, our mission is to make enterprise cloud infrastructure accessible and affordable.

## 2. Pricing Plans
We offer three main pricing tiers:
- **Starter Plan ($29/month)**: Includes 2 CPU cores, 4GB RAM, 100GB SSD storage, and 1TB bandwidth. Ideal for small projects and staging environments.
- **Professional Plan ($99/month)**: Includes 8 CPU cores, 16GB RAM, 500GB NVMe storage, and unmetered bandwidth. Best for growing production applications.
- **Enterprise Plan (Custom Pricing)**: Dedicated hardware, 24/7 priority phone support, SLA guarantee of 99.99%, and custom cloud architecture consulting.

## 3. Support & SLA
- Standard support is available via email at support@apexcloud.example.com. Expected response time is within 24 hours.
- Professional plan users get access to live chat support with a 2-hour response time.
- Enterprise customers receive a dedicated account manager and emergency phone support.
- Our SLA guarantees 99.9% uptime for Starter and Professional plans. If uptime drops below this, customers are eligible for service credits.

## 4. Refund & Cancellation Policy
- **30-Day Money-Back Guarantee**: All new customers are eligible for a full refund within the first 30 days of their initial purchase, no questions asked.
- **Cancellations**: Subscriptions can be canceled at any time from the billing dashboard. Service will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle. We do not offer prorated refunds for partial months.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Do you offer free trials?**
A: Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial on our Starter and Professional plans. No credit card is required to sign up.

**Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later?**
A: Absolutely. You can change your plan at any time from the account settings. Upgrades take effect immediately, while downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

**Q: Where are your data centers located?**
A: We currently have data centers in New York (US-East), San Francisco (US-West), London (UK), and Singapore (Asia-Pacific). You can choose your preferred region during server deployment.

**Q: Do you support automatic backups?**
A: Yes, daily automated backups are included for free on the Professional and Enterprise plans. Starter plan users can add automated backups for an additional $5/month.
